About the Show

Decode the DNA of Indian Office Dynamics

The Investigation Lens podcast goes beyond theory — each episode unpacks real patterns in workplace conflict, investigation practice, and ethical decision-making in Indian organisations.

Whether you are an HR professional, an IC member, a manager navigating a complaint, or simply curious about how workplace justice works, this podcast gives you the tools and perspective to act with confidence.
