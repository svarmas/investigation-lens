About the Show

Responding Well to Workplace Issues

Investigation Lens is a podcast about responding well to workplace issues. Practical, grounded perspectives on resolution pathways, workplace investigations, PoSH, and building HR capability — one clear idea at a time.

The focus is on helping organisations and people-leaders understand what's happening, choose the right approach, and handle difficult situations with confidence and fairness.
