Services & Training

A Progressive Learning Journey

Our training system guides participants from foundational knowledge to advanced, practical mastery, with measurable outcomes at each stage to ensure skill transfer and organisational impact.

Tier 1

Foundational Modules

Establish core concepts and baseline skills essential for all participants.

Objectives

  • Equip participants to establish and maintain compliant, effective Internal Committee (IC) and internal investigation teams
  • Ensure understanding and practical application of key workforce and workplace policies
  • Build foundational knowledge of workplace conflict, psychological safety, and de-escalation

Outcomes

  • Participants can correctly outline IC setup steps and apply compliance protocols in simulated settings
  • Ability to communicate and apply key workforce policies with confidence
  • Improved understanding of interpersonal dynamics and trust in team settings

Target Participants

P&C teams, IC members, Internal Employee Relations Investigation Teams, Managers and Team Leaders

Tier 2

Advanced Sessions

Deepen expertise through interactive, scenario-based learning, trainer-delivered for maximum impact and practical reinforcement.

Example Modules

  • Early Dispute Resolution (EDR) Skills Training
  • Mediation: Procedural & Skill-Based Training
  • Investigation Training: Process, Documentation & Interviewing Skills
  • 30-Minute Follow-Up Sessions to reinforce learning, address questions, and assess retention

Coaching & Action-Based Learning

Enable immediate application of skills to live or practice scenarios, supporting ongoing capability building and problem-solving.

Target Participants

P&C teams, Managers and Team Leaders
